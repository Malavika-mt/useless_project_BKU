### Project Description
A wearable tech accessory designed to reflect the wearer's current mood. Users can manually set their emotional state via a companion dashboard, which is then provided as text-based visual feedback on the clip.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Verbally expressing your emotions is exhausting, and people constantly asking "How are you?" is highly inefficient. Furthermore, the backs of our heads are completely devoid of any conversational status indicators. 

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
A completely necessary electronic hair accessory where you manually log into a web dashboard to set your mood, just so a screen in your hair can broadcast it as text to anyone standing behind you. 

For Hardware:
- 1x ESP32 Mini
- 1x Standard metal or plastic hair clip
- 1x Miniaturized LiPo battery
- Soldering iron and thin gauge wire
